Country research-output percentile against country research-need percentile, 2000-2026
publication years, 193 countries plotted of the 195 in the spatial layer. The need percentile is
the rank-weighted need index stored as need_rank_pct and rescaled here from 0-1 to 0-100; the
research percentile is the percentile rank of the fractional eligible study count, and countries
with zero coded attention share the lowest rank. Colour encodes research percentile minus need
percentile, in percentile points, on a diverging scale centred on zero: orange marks a country
ranked lower for research than for need, blue the reverse. The shaded quadrant holds countries
at or above the median for need and at or below the median for research, and contains 48
countries; the ten with the largest shortfall are listed inside it, since every country there
sits at the same research percentile. 71 of the 193 plotted countries tie at the lowest research
percentile, 18.97, because none has an eligible study. 2 countries have no need index and are
not plotted; they are kept in the saved data with NA.
