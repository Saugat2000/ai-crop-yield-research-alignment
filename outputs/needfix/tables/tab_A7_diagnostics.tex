\begin{tablenotes}
\item[] Panel A gives the year of the most recent research and development expenditure observation for each estimation country. Panel B compares the reported cluster-robust standard errors with quasi-Poisson standard errors formed from the Pearson dispersion, $\hat\phi_{P} = 286$. The deviance-based dispersion for the same fit is $\hat\phi_{D} = 1.4$. The two disagree by more than two orders of magnitude because the Pearson statistic is dominated by a small number of systems with very large counts, so a constant variance-to-mean ratio does not describe these data and the quasi-Poisson interval is conservative rather than correct. Cluster-robust errors, which assume no variance function, remain the reported choice. Panel C reports Lagrange Multiplier tests for a spatial lag and a spatial error process; none rejects at the 5\% level.
